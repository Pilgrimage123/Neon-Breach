# NEON BREACH — Hero Core Fantasies

> **Design Rule:** This document captures **only a brief outline of each hero's main fantasy and defining mechanical pillars**. Secondary numbers, starting Credit/Memory tweaks, Overdrive values, and non-core parameters are intentionally left open so AI and balance passes can tune them freely without violating the hero's identity.

---

## 1. Auraline — *Slow Juggernaut*
* **Core Fantasy:** An unstoppable, heavy-plated vanguard who shrugs off early punishment through sheer durability and turns deadweight malfunctions into draw momentum.
* **Defining Pillars:**
  * **High Vitality & Starting Combat Armor:** Enters every combat with high Max HP and a significant persistent **Armor** pool that absorbs hits across rounds beneath temporary Shield.
  * **One Dud in Each Color:** Starts with 1 **Dud** tile (no base effect, still carries color for Surges/Overdrive) in Attack, Defense, and TECH.
  * **Dud-to-UpCharge Engine:** Gains **+1 UpCharge** (draws +1 extra tile next turn) for every Dud she resolves—giving her a natural engine to cycle through Duds rather than feeling forced to swamp or purge them with purchased AI Skill Chips.
