# NEON BREACH — Hero Core Fantasies

> **Design Rule:** This document captures **only the qualitative concept, core fantasy, and defining mechanical identity of each Hero**. It intentionally excludes concrete stats, tile counts, damage values, cooldown numbers, and Memory budgets so the roster can be continuously balanced and rebalanced in [`BALANCE_GUIDE.md`](./BALANCE_GUIDE.md) and `src/engine/data.ts` without drifting from the core fantasy.

---

## 1. Kira (`KIRA-09`) — *Agile Tempo Striker*
* **Core Fantasy:** A high-speed cyber-acrobat who seizes the initiative with chained attack turns, weaves evasive vault dodges out of full-Shield blocks, and builds explosive momentum on held tiles.
* **Conceptual Pillars:**
  * **Blitz Reflex (Attack Tempo Chain):** Committing to an Attack turn grants an immediate bonus turn on a short cooldown, letting Kira burst a priority target and follow up with a defensive guard or tech setup before Mutants can respond.
  * **Evasive Dodge (Defensive Counter-Tempo):** Blocking an incoming Mutant strike completely with Shield triggers an acrobatic backward vault dodge that accelerates her Blitz Reflex cooldown.
  * **Cyber-Momentum (Board Patience):** Unresolved tiles kept on the Board across turns charge with extra offensive and defensive force, and her Overdrive charges primarily through aggressive Attack execution.
  * **Overdrive (`Energy Dropkick`):** An acrobatic flying dropkick that slams the frontline Mutant and knocks it backward into the formation, detonates an energy shockwave across all Mutants (including the frontline target), and deploys a reflex Shield.

## 2. Auraline (`AURALINE-07`) — *Armored Juggernaut*
* **Core Fantasy:** An unstoppable, heavy-plated vanguard who weathers attrition behind persistent plating and converts reactor malfunctions into massive draw capacity and reinforced armor.
* **Conceptual Pillars:**
  * **Persistent Juggernaut Armor:** Enters every battle encased in heavy persistent Armor beneath her temporary Shield, backed by the highest vitality and chip Memory capacity in the roster.
  * **Reactor Core Malfunctions (Dud-to-UpCharge & Plating):** Starts with unstable Dud tiles across all three colors. Resolving a Dud converts the malfunction into extra next-turn Board draw capacity (UpCharge) and re-plates her persistent Armor.
  * **Permanent Reactor Origin:** Upgrading her innate Duds at Dynasty facilities (Charter Academy, Stork Nursery, or FabLab) turns them into powerful active tiles while preserving their Reactor Core origin so her engine grows stronger rather than turning off.
  * **Overdrive (`Sovereign Broadside`):** A heavy artillery barrage across all Mutants that re-plates her persistent Armor and banks extra UpCharge draws.

## 3. Zuri (`ZURI-03`) — *Solar Swarm Commander*
* **Core Fantasy:** An airborne Afrofuturist drone commander hovering above the sky-bridge who orchestrates synchronized tri-drone support pulses by weaving between tile colors and cycling the fastest Overdrive in the game.
* **Conceptual Pillars:**
  * **Tri-Drone Sync (Color-Weaving Command):** Accompanied by three autonomous drones — Solar Hawk (Attack), Aegis Walker (Defense), and Med-Rotor (TECH). Resolving any tile color primes the matching drone; switching to a different color on the following turn triggers the primed drone's support pulse alongside the new action.
  * **Solar Capacitor (TECH Overdrive Engine):** Draws deepest into TECH utility and charges Overdrive rapidly from TECH tiles, turning support turns into frequent ultimate activations.
  * **Innate Armor Shred & Aerial Command:** Brings built-in multi-target armor stripping and hybrid shield-boost telemetry straight out of her starting kit.
  * **Overdrive (`Solar Swarm Protocol`):** Strips Mutant Armor across the sky-bridge before unleashing a coordinated swarm bombardment, projecting a barrier, and cleansing corrosive buildup.
