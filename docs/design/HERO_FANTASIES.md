# NEON BREACH — Hero Core Fantasies

> **Design Rule:** This document captures **only a brief outline of each Hero's main fantasy and defining mechanical pillars**. Secondary numbers, starting Credit/Memory tweaks, Overdrive values, and non-core parameters are left open for balance passes without violating the Hero's identity.

---

## 1. Auraline (`AURALINE-07`) — *Armored Juggernaut*
* **Core Fantasy:** An unstoppable, heavy-plated vanguard who shrugs off early punishment through sheer durability and turns deadweight malfunctions into draw momentum.
* **Defining Pillars:**
  * **High Vitality & Starting Armor:** Enters every combat with high Max HP (`78 HP`) and a large persistent **Armor** pool (`18`) that absorbs hits across Turns beneath temporary Shield, backed by a generous `14` Memory budget.
  * **One Dud in Each Color:** Starts with `1` **Dud** Tile (`0` base value, still carries color for Stack Surges and Overdrive) in Attack, Defense, and TECH (`6` Innate Tiles total).
  * **Dud-to-UpCharge Engine:** Gains **`+1` UpCharge** (draws `+1` extra Tile on the next Turn's Board, up to `9`) for every Dud resolved—giving her a natural engine to cycle through Duds rather than feeling forced to purge them immediately.
  * **Overdrive (`Sovereign Broadside`):** Deals `20` Armor-piercing DMG to all Mutants, restores `+10` Armor, and banks `+2` UpCharge.

---

## 2. Kira (`KIRA-09`) — *Agile Tempo Striker*
* **Core Fantasy:** A glass-cannon cyber-acrobat who weaves agile Dodges, holds unresolved Tiles on the Board to charge momentum, and converts fast TECH setups into explosive Overdrive chains.
* **Defining Pillars:**
  * **Cyber-Momentum on Held Tiles:** Every **Held Tile** (`isHeldFromPrevTurn`) gains **`+1` Attack / `+1` Defense** when resolved, rewarding patient Board setup across Turns.
  * **Fast Overdrive & Tech Surge Opening:** Starts with `4` TECH Tiles across her `9`-Tile starting pool (`5` Innate Tiles + *Predictive Aim.AI* & *Auto-Suture.AI*) and gains **`+20%` Overdrive** per TECH Tile resolved.
  * **Hybrid Pistol / Dash-Kick Combat:** Alternates ranged *Predictive Aim* bolts with acrobatic close-quarters Dash-Kicks and mid-air **Dodge** vaults on evaded/negated strikes (`58 HP`, tight `12` Memory).
  * **Overdrive (`Plasma Blitz`):** Deals `20` Armor-piercing DMG to all Mutants, grants `+30%` DMG Boost, and builds `+6` Shield (`0 AP`).

---

## 3. Zuri (`ZURI-03`) — *Solar Swarm Commander*
* **Core Fantasy:** An airborne drone tactician hovering above the sky-bridge who shreds Mutant Armor with coordinated Tri-Drone volleys and fuels the fastest Overdrive cycle in the roster.
* **Defining Pillars:**
  * **Fastest TECH Overdrive Uplink:** Gains **`+25%` Overdrive** per TECH Tile resolved—unlocking rapid *Solar Swarm Protocol* cycles even against armored squads.
  * **Innate Armor Shred & Hybrid Utility:** Starts with balanced `3/3/3` color coverage (`62 HP`, `13` Memory, `50 CR`, `5` Innate Tiles + *Swarm Vector.AI* & *Sanctuary Mesh.AI*), bringing built-in **Armor Shred (`-8` Armor All + `3` Pierce)** and **Tactical Shield (`+4` Shield + `+24%` DMG)**.
  * **Tri-Drone Formation:** Attack, Shield, and Med drones autonomously orbit and engage targets directly from formation without requiring melee closes.
  * **Overdrive (`Solar Swarm Protocol`):** Deals `16` Armor-piercing DMG to all Mutants, shreds `-12` Armor across all Mutants, grants `+8` Shield, and cleanses `2` Corrode stacks.
